Privacy Policy

Lit Lines - Wire Puzzle · Effective date: November 26, 2026 · 한국어

CloverArc (“we” or “the developer”) explains in this policy what personal information Lit Lines - Wire Puzzle (“the app”) processes and why, which third-party services it uses, how long information is kept and deleted, and how you can exercise your rights.

This policy applies to the app’s game service, advertising, in-app purchases and customer support.

1. Information we process and why

The app does not require sign-up or login, and it does not send your game data to any server operated by the developer. The following information is processed when the app uses third-party services such as advertising and in-app purchases.

A. Device and advertising information

  • Information: advertising ID, device model, operating system and app version, language setting, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad request, impression, click and view records, ad performance and diagnostic information
  • Purpose: showing ads, providing relevant ads, measuring ad performance, confirming rewards for rewarded ads, preventing ad fraud and abuse
  • How: processed automatically by the advertising SDKs included in the app (Section 3)

Whether the advertising ID is available and how it is used depends on your operating system settings, your choices and applicable law.

B. In-app purchase information

  • Information: product ID, transaction ID, purchase token or receipt, and the purchase, delivery and restore status needed to verify a purchase
  • Purpose: processing purchases, delivering products, preventing duplicate delivery, restoring purchases and handling payment inquiries
  • When: when you buy an in-app product or use the restore purchases feature

Payment details are entered and approved through the Google Play billing system, or the ONE store billing system for the version distributed on ONE store. The developer never receives your full card number, card password or other payment method details.

C. Customer support information

  • Information: email address, the content of your inquiry and any attachments you choose to send
  • Purpose: answering inquiries, investigating issues, handling privacy requests and resolving disputes
  • How: collected when you email the developer

Please do not include passwords, full card numbers, national ID numbers or other information that is not needed to handle your inquiry.

2. Game data stored on your device

The app stores your stage progress, the wire layout of a stage in progress, coins and items (hints and compasses), purchased products and ad-removal status, sound and vibration settings, and the number of ad rewards used on your device. The save file is encrypted.

Data stored on your device is not sent to the developer.

You can delete this data by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. Some data may be restored after reinstalling through your operating system’s backup feature.

3. Advertising and your choices

The app shows Google AdMob interstitial and rewarded ads directly and through Unity LevelPlay mediation, and shows Unity Ads and ironSource Ads through Unity LevelPlay mediation.

The information described in Section 1.A is processed to provide ads, and related information may be shared among Unity, Google and partners that take part in delivering and measuring ads.

You can manage ad-related data processing in the following ways.

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in the privacy or ads menu of your Android settings.
  • Use the privacy settings or notices shown with an ad, where available.
  • In regions where consent is required, a consent screen provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) is shown before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with the ad privacy settings button shown in the app’s settings in those regions.

Limiting personalized ads may still show non-personalized ads, and it does not stop all basic processing needed to deliver ads.

The ad-removal product removes interstitial ads. Rewarded ads that you choose to watch (getting a hint, doubling a reward, continuing, free coins and similar) remain available.

For more information, see:

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google advertising

4. In-app purchases

The app uses the Google Play billing system and Unity IAP to process and restore in-app purchases. The version distributed on ONE store uses the ONE store billing system and the ONE store in-app purchase SDK instead.

Purchase information is used to confirm delivery, prevent duplicate delivery, restore purchases and resolve payment issues.

Payment and transaction information processed by Google is subject to Google’s privacy policy and payment policies, and information processed by ONE store is subject to ONE store’s. For more information, see Unity IAP privacy information.

8. Deletion

Personal information managed by the developer is deleted without delay when its retention period ends or its purpose is achieved.

  • Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered.
  • Any paper records are shredded or otherwise destroyed appropriately.
  • Information stored by third-party services is deleted under each service’s deletion features, request procedures and backup cycles.

9. Your rights

Within the scope of applicable law, you can request access to, correction, deletion or restriction of processing of your personal information, and withdraw consent. Requests can also be made through a legal guardian or authorized agent.

Please send requests to cloverarcgames@gmail.com.

The developer verifies the minimum information needed and handles requests under applicable law. Because the developer does not operate user accounts or server-side game data, you can delete the game data on your device yourself by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Information managed directly by Google or Unity may require using that provider’s privacy settings or request procedures. The developer will point you to them where needed.

10. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the GDPR and similar local laws apply, and the developer processes personal information on the following bases.

  • Performance of a contract: providing the game service, processing in-app purchases and delivering products
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention and responding to inquiries
  • Consent: processing that legally requires consent, such as personalized advertising. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

In addition to the rights in Section 9, you have the right to data portability and to object to processing, and you can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Transfers outside these regions rely on safeguards adopted by each provider, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Users in the United States

This section applies to residents of U.S. states with consumer privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA.

A. Categories of personal information collected

In the past 12 months, the app collected the following categories of personal information from the sources and for the purposes described in Sections 1 to 4.

  • Identifiers: advertising ID, IP address, and email address when you contact us
  • Commercial information: in-app purchase records such as product and transaction IDs
  • Internet or other electronic network activity: ad request, impression, click and view records and ad diagnostic information
  • Approximate location: approximate location derived from the IP address

The developer does not collect precise location and does not use or disclose sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

B. Sale, sharing and targeted advertising

The developer does not sell personal information for money. However, allowing the advertising partners in Section 3 to collect device and advertising identifiers and ad usage information in the app to show and measure ads may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, or “targeted advertising”, under some state laws. For this purpose, identifiers, network activity and approximate location are provided to those partners. Payment providers act as service providers processing information on the developer’s behalf.

You can opt out of such sale, sharing or targeted advertising in the following ways.

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID and turn off ad personalization in your Android privacy or ads settings.
  • Use the ad privacy settings button shown in the app’s settings in applicable regions.
  • Email cloverarcgames@gmail.com with the subject “Do Not Sell or Share”. We will tell you how your request was handled.

The developer does not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

C. Your rights

Depending on your state, you may have the right to know and access the personal information we collected, to request deletion or correction, to receive it in a portable format, and to opt out as described above. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Please send requests to cloverarcgames@gmail.com. Requests can also be made through an authorized agent. We will respond within 45 days after verifying the request with information related to the app, and will let you know if we need up to 45 more days. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond within the period required by your state’s law.

12. Security and children’s privacy

The developer limits access to personal information to what is necessary and applies administrative and technical safeguards suited to the service, such as protection in transit, encryption of data saved on the device, access control and security updates.

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not primarily directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child’s personal information was processed without a guardian’s consent, we will stop the processing and delete it as required by law.

Please contact the developer with questions or deletion requests regarding children’s personal information.
