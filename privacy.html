Privacy Policy

Blocks - Puzzle Escape · Effective date: October 30, 2026 · 한국어 · Terms of Service

CloverArc (“we”, “us” or the “Developer”) respects the privacy of users of Blocks - Puzzle Escape (the “App”). This policy explains what personal information the App processes and why, which third-party services are used, how long information is kept and how it is deleted, and what rights you have and how to exercise them.

This policy applies to the App’s game service, advertising, in-app purchases, Google Play Games integration and customer support.

1. Information We Process and Why

The App does not require you to create an account with us. However, the following information is processed to operate the game and use third-party services.

A. Device and usage information

  • Information: device model, operating system and app version, language setting, IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, app installation and instance identifiers, advertising ID, and similar information
  • Purpose: providing the service and checking compatibility, analyzing usage, serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud and abuse
  • How: processed automatically by the App and the third-party SDKs it contains

Whether the advertising ID is available and how it is used depends on your device settings, your choices and applicable law.

B. Gameplay and analytics information

  • Information: app launches and screen navigation; stage start, clear, fail and retry records; play time; stars earned; items and in-game currency earned and spent; event participation; ad views and rewards granted; shop usage; purchase results; and setting changes such as language and theme
  • Purpose: understanding how the game is played, improving difficulty and features, verifying rewards, improving service quality
  • How: event and statistical analysis through Firebase Analytics

C. Crash and diagnostic information

  • Information: crash and exception records, time of the error, device, OS and app state at the time of the error, app installation identifier, the screen or stage related to the error, and diagnostic logs
  • Purpose: finding the cause of errors, responding to outages, improving app stability
  • How: automatic crash reporting through Firebase Crashlytics

For details on information Firebase collects automatically, see Firebase data disclosure.

D. Google Play Games information

  • Information: player ID, player display name, sign-in status, leaderboard scores and ranks, and game data saved to the cloud
  • Cloud save data: stage progress and clear records, stars earned, currency and items owned, product and ad-removal status, tutorial records, theme and event progress, and similar data
  • Purpose: linking your account, saving, restoring and syncing game progress, providing leaderboards
  • When: when you sign in to Google Play Games or use its features

E. In-app purchase information

  • Information: product ID, transaction ID, purchase token or receipt, and the purchase, delivery and restore status needed to verify purchases
  • Purpose: processing purchases, delivering products, checking purchase history, restoring purchases and handling payment inquiries
  • When: when you buy an in-app product or restore purchases

Payment details are entered and approved through the Google Play billing system. We never receive your full card number, card password or other payment method details.

F. Customer support information

  • Information: email address, the content of your inquiry and any attachments you choose to send
  • Purpose: answering inquiries, investigating errors, handling privacy requests and resolving disputes
  • How: collected when you email us

Please do not send information that is not needed for your inquiry, such as passwords, full card numbers or national ID numbers.

2. Game Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores game progress, currency and items, settings and event records on your device.

Data stored only on your device is not sent to us simply by being stored. However, if you use Google Play Games, some game data is saved to the cloud, and some gameplay information is used for analytics or crash reporting as described in Section 1.

You can delete on-device data by clearing the App’s data in your device settings. Some data may be restored after reinstalling the App through OS backups or cloud saves.

3. Advertising and Your Choices

The App shows interstitial and rewarded ads from Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and Google AdMob through Unity LevelPlay mediation.

To serve ads, the following information is processed: device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, ad request, impression, click and view records, and ad performance and diagnostic information.

This information is used to display ads, show relevant ads, measure ad performance, verify rewards, and prevent ad fraud and abuse. Related information may be shared among Unity, Google and partners that take part in delivering ads.

You can manage ad-related processing as follows:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device’s privacy or ads settings.
  • Use privacy controls or notices shown within ads, where available.
  • Where consent is required and a consent screen is provided, make or change your choices through that screen or the privacy settings.

Limiting personalized ads may still result in non-personalized ads being shown, and does not stop all basic processing needed to deliver ads.

Ad-removal products apply to the ads stated in the product description. Purchasing ad removal does not stop other processing such as analytics or crash reporting.

For more information:

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information for advertising

4. Google Play Games and Public Leaderboards

Google Play Games uses authentication and game services provided by Google. We never receive your Google account password.

For signed-in users, game progress and scores are processed by Google Play Games to provide cloud saves and leaderboards. Your player display name, scores and rank on leaderboards may be visible to other users depending on Google Play Games visibility settings.

Disconnecting your account in the App’s settings stops linking and syncing. Disconnecting or deleting the App does not automatically delete existing cloud data or leaderboard records.

To delete game data stored by Google, see Delete your Google Play Games data.

Deleting Google Play Games data does not delete data processed separately by us or other third-party services.

5. In-App Purchases

The App uses the Google Play billing system and Unity IAP to process and restore purchases.

Purchase information is used to confirm delivery, prevent duplicate delivery, restore purchases and resolve payment issues. Some purchase results and product information are also used for usage analytics.

Payment and transaction information processed by Google is subject to Google’s privacy policy and payment policies. For details, see Unity IAP privacy information.

9. Deletion Procedure

When the retention period ends or the purpose is achieved, we delete personal information we manage without delay.

  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that makes recovery difficult.
  • Any paper records are destroyed by shredding or another appropriate method.
  • Information stored by third-party services is deleted through their deletion features, request procedures and backup deletion cycles.

10. Your Rights

To the extent provided by applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information, and withdraw consent. Requests may also be made through a legal guardian or authorized representative.

Please send requests to cloverarcgames@gmail.com.

We will verify the minimum information needed to handle your request and act in accordance with applicable law. If we cannot confirm that the data belongs to you, or the law requires us to limit the request, we will tell you why and what we can do.

Information managed directly by Google or Unity may require you to use that company’s privacy settings or request process. We will guide you where needed.

Deleting personal information or disconnecting your account may limit some features such as cloud restore, leaderboards and purchase verification.

11. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the GDPR and other local data protection laws apply. We process personal information on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: providing the game, cloud saves, processing in-app purchases and delivering products
  • Legitimate interests: crash analysis, service improvement, security and fraud prevention
  • Consent: processing that legally requires consent, such as personalized advertising. You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

In addition to the rights in Section 10, you have the right to data portability and the right to object to processing, and you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country. Transfers outside these regions rely on safeguards adopted by each provider, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Security and Children’s Privacy

We limit access to personal information to what is necessary and apply administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the service, such as protecting data in transit, managing access rights and applying security updates.

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child’s personal information has been processed without required parental consent, we will stop processing and delete it as required by law.

Please contact us with any questions or deletion requests regarding children’s information.
